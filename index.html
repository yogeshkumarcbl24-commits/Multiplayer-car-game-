How to Play
Drive

←→ (or AD, or drag) to steer, ↑ for gas, ↓ to brake or reverse. The car only turns when you steer it — it won't auto-follow curves for you, so keep steering through every bend or you'll drift wide.

Fight

Ram other cars to bump them off their line, or hold Space to fire your mounted gun. Watch your HP bar — run out and your car explodes, then respawns a moment later.

Race

Cross the finish line banner before time runs out to post a time. Pick a longer race length and the finish line sits farther out, so the race actually takes about that long either way.

Modes

Practice Solo is a free drive with no timer. Race vs Computer is a timed race against an AI opponent, no other players needed. In multiplayer, create a room and share the code with friends on the same WiFi — only the room's host picks the race length and starts it, everyone else just joins in.
